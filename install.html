SCE

Slime Clothing Extension: more clothes for Bondage Club, with many options and colours. Made by SlimeQueenSamantha.

🐒 Userscript (loads by itself)

With Violentmonkey or Tampermonkey: install SCE. It starts with the game every time, and updates itself.

🔖 Bookmark (no extension needed)

  1. Show your bookmarks bar (Ctrl + Shift + B).
  2. Drag this button onto it:
    👘 SCE
  3. Open Bondage Club and click the SCE bookmark before you log in, so outfits with SCE clothes load with you.

Click it each time you open the game. It always loads the newest SCE, so there's never anything to update. Clicking it twice does nothing extra.

Can't drag? (phones, some browsers)

Make a new bookmark of any page, edit it, and paste this as its address (URL):

⚠ It must start with javascript:. Some browsers (Chrome) drop that word when you paste; if it's missing, type it back at the very start, or the browser just searches for the code. On a phone, open the bookmark by typing its name in the address bar and tapping the bookmark in the suggestions.

👘 Wearing the clothes

Open your wardrobe: SCE's clothes have the slime kimono badge in the corner. Click one again for its options (style, sleeves, pattern...) and use the paint brush for the colours.

Other players see them when they have SCE too.
